Margreth Pre & Primary School

Joining Instructions

Welcome to Margreth Pre & Primary School. These instructions explain how to apply, which documents to prepare, and the term fees for each level. Please read them and keep a copy for reference.

Classes: Nursery (Pre-Primary, age 3) to Standard 7. Applications are accepted throughout the academic year, subject to available places.

Admission Process

  1. Get and complete the application form. Apply online or collect a physical form from the school office. Complete the student and parent/guardian details and indicate the class requested.
  2. Prepare the supporting documents. Check each item in the requirements list below. Previous school reports apply to learners who have attended another school; a transfer certificate is required for Standard 2–7.
  3. Submit the application. Send or take the completed form and required documents to the school for review.
  4. Attend the interview and assessment. The school will arrange a session with the family to help determine suitable placement for the child.
  5. Wait for admission confirmation. Applications are subject to availability. The school will communicate the admission decision and next steps.
  6. Complete enrollment. After admission is confirmed, pay the applicable term fees and complete the enrollment process with the school.

Admission Requirements

  • Copy of the child's birth certificate
  • Previous school reports, if applicable
  • Transfer certificate for Standard 2–7
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form
  • Copy of the parent/guardian's ID

After Admission Confirmation

Follow the instructions given by the school, pay the fee for the child's level, and complete enrollment. Contact the school if you need help with the application, required documents, assessment, or payment information.
