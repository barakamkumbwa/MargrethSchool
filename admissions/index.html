Begin your child's journey to success

Welcome to Margreth Pre & Primary School! We are excited that you are considering us for your child's education. Our admission process is simple, transparent, and designed to ensure the best fit for your child and our school community. We welcome students from Nursery (Age 3) to Standard 7, and we accept applications throughout the academic year subject to availability.

01

Fill Application Form

Complete the online application form or collect a physical form from our school office.

02

Submit Documents

Provide required documents including birth certificate, previous academic records, and passport photos.

03

Interview & Assessment

Attend a brief interview and assessment session to ensure proper placement for your child.

04

Confirmation & Enrollment

Receive admission confirmation, pay fees, and complete enrollment to officially join our school.

Admission Requirements

  • Birth Certificate (Copy)
  • Previous School Reports (if applicable)
  • Transfer Certificate (for Std 2-7)
  • Two Passport-Size Photographs
  • Filled Application Form
  • Parent/Guardian ID Copy

School Fees Structure

Level Per Term (TZS)
Pre-Primary (Nursery) 500,000
Standard 1 - 3 650,000
Standard 4 - 7 800,000

Fees include tuition, meals, and basic learning materials. Transport is charged separately.
