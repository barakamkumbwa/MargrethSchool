About Our School

Margreth Pre & Primary School

Margreth Pre & Primary School was founded with a vision to provide quality education that nurtures young minds physically, intellectually, and morally. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted institution dedicated to academic excellence and character development.

Our school is built on the principles of discipline, hard work, and a commitment to serving our community. We believe that every child deserves a strong foundation to succeed in life.

Our mission is to provide a holistic education that develops the intellectual, social, emotional, and physical abilities of every child. We strive to create a nurturing environment where students can discover their talents and reach their full potential.

  • To deliver high-quality education that meets national standards.
  • To instill moral values and discipline in every student.
  • To prepare students for lifelong learning and responsible citizenship.

To be a leading primary school in Tanzania, recognized for academic excellence, character development, and community service. We envision a generation of confident, creative, and compassionate leaders.

"Nurturing Young Minds for Global Success"

This motto reflects our commitment to preparing students not just for exams, but for life. We believe that a strong foundation in education and character is the key to success in the modern world.

What is the admission process?

Our admission process is simple and straightforward. Parents are required to fill out an application form, provide previous academic records (if applicable), and submit a copy of the child's birth certificate. Once submitted, we will schedule a brief interview and assessment to ensure the best placement for your child. We welcome students throughout the academic year subject to availability.
