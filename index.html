About Our School

Margreth Pre & Primary School

Location
Margreth Pre & Primary School is located in Songambele, Kongwa District, Dodoma Region, Tanzania.

About
Margreth Pre & Primary School provides quality education from pre-primary to Standard VII. We are committed to academic excellence, discipline, creativity, and character development in a safe and supportive learning environment. Our goal is to nurture confident, responsible, and capable learners for a bright future.

Vision: To nurture knowledgeable, disciplined, creative, and responsible learners who are prepared to contribute positively to their communities and build a bright future.

Mission: To provide quality education from pre-primary to Standard VII in a safe and supportive environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, creativity, and character development for every learner.

Why Choose Us?

Our core values guide every child to learn, grow, and build a bright future.

Academic Excellence

We provide quality education from pre-primary to Standard VII and help learners build strong foundations for lifelong learning.

Discipline and Responsibility

We encourage respect, self-discipline, and responsibility so learners can make positive choices in school and in their communities.

Creativity and Curiosity

We nurture curiosity, creative thinking, and problem-solving through engaging lessons and opportunities to explore new ideas.

Godly Values

We nurture every child in godly values such as love, honesty, respect, kindness, and responsibility, helping them grow into caring and principled individuals.

A Safe and Supportive Environment

We create a welcoming place where every child feels supported, can participate, build friendships, and enjoy learning.

We welcome families who share our commitment to helping every learner thrive.
